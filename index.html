Executive Summary

The Port Charlotte Town Center, strategically located at 1441 Tamiami Trail, represents one of Southwest Florida's most significant redevelopment opportunities. This ±54.20-acre property, currently zoned for General Large Scale Commercial use, offers approximately [835] 1,000,000 square feet of retail space with exceptional highway access and visibility.

Current Status

Owned by WP Glimcher, marketed for redevelopment with approved 250-unit apartment complex and potential for 722 total units [711].

Market Value

Commercial properties average $339,728 ($231/sq ft) with assessed value of $25.2M for the mall property [566].

Demographics

Population ~66,571, median age 55-60, household income ~$60,000-$66,000 [822].

Originally developed in 1989 by the Edward J. DeBartolo Corporation, the property has transitioned through multiple ownerships, most recently acquired by bondholders in 2022. The site's redevelopment potential is enhanced by Charlotte County's 2020 mixed-use zoning category, allowing for greater density and building height [877].
